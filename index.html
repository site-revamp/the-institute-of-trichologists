Established in 1902

A professional home for the science of hair and scalp.

The Institute of Trichologists is a professional association dedicated to the development of trichology and the standards of those who practise it.

Through education, registration and continuing professional development, the Institute supports qualified practitioners and helps the public understand the role of trichology in hair and scalp health.

Its work brings together professional learning, ethical practice and public-facing information in a specialist field concerned with the structure, function and conditions of the hair and scalp.

Supporting the profession at every level.

Continuing development

Supporting ongoing learning and professional development for members of the Institute.

International community

Connecting students and trichologists across the UK and an international professional community.

Education & training

Develop the knowledge to make a difference in trichology.

The Institute's Level 5 Diploma in Clinical Trichology combines academic study with clinical learning to prepare students for professional practice.

The course is structured across foundation, intermediate and final levels. It is delivered mainly through home-based distance learning, with compulsory clinical training sessions in London.
