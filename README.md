Website Overview
The Institute of Trichologists (IOT) is a UK professional association established in 1902, dedicated to professional trichology education, practitioner registration, clinical standards, and hair and scalp health. Its audience includes aspiring trichologists, qualified practitioners, students, and members of the public seeking reliable professional information.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the Institute as an established professional authority, making its educational programmes, professional registration, and public resources easier to discover.
Main Improvements
1. Clearer Business Identity and Positioning
The homepage places greater emphasis on the Institute's role as a professional association, distinguishing its educational, registration, and regulatory responsibilities from its clinical presence on Harley Street.

2. Improved Service and Education Presentation
Professional registration, the Level 5 Diploma in Clinical Trichology, continuing professional development, and clinical training are presented more clearly to help prospective students and practitioners understand the Institute's offerings.

3. Better Information Structure and Navigation
Important information and audience-specific pathways are organised more clearly, helping students, registered professionals, and members of the public find relevant information without navigating through unrelated content.
Design Improvements
- Visual Hierarchy: Clearer content organisation gives greater prominence to the Institute's professional identity and primary offerings.
- Layout: Improved content structure makes educational programmes, registration information, and professional resources easier to explore.
- User Experience: More clearly defined information pathways support different visitor needs.
- Consistency: A more cohesive presentation strengthens the Institute's established professional identity.
- Overall Appearance: The homepage adopts a more structured, professional direction appropriate for a long-established UK professional association.
Project Objective
The redesign aims to improve how The Institute of Trichologists communicates its professional authority, educational opportunities, and registration services while making essential information more accessible to students, practitioners, and the public.
